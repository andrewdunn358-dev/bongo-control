Removed: the function, its call site and import in Settings, and the
now-unused COMPOSITION_COCKPITS export. No cockpit-based restriction
replaces it.

The three assertions that existed only to enforce it are replaced by
assertions of the invariant that makes it unnecessary: a theme's own
composition wins over the base it extends, a theme without one inherits
that base, and an unmigrated appearance resolves to undefined rather
than an empty layout. 'adventure' is the discriminating case and is
noted as such in the test - it is the only name with a built-in to lose
to, so inverting the precedence leaves the other two passing.

cockpit is documented, in schema.ts, builtins/index.ts and
theme_service.py, as temporary compatibility and inheritance metadata
that nothing below Validated Composition may consult. Not renamed.

Geometry unchanged: all 18 measured cells identical to the pre-removal
Stage 1 build.
