Your van's mission control

VanOS is an open-source, plugin-based dashboard operating system for campervans. Live battery, solar, weather, nearby places, camera and mission intelligence — all on a Raspberry Pi, all in one calm aurora cockpit.

What it does

Every panel is driven by real hardware on a van — or by a realistic simulation when there's none connected, which is exactly what you're seeing in the demo.

🔋

Live telemetry

Battery voltage and solar production stream over a single WebSocket from a real Victron SmartSolar MPPT over Bluetooth — or simulated when off-grid of hardware.

☀️

Solar intelligence

A weather-driven solar outlook plus a "good / normal / low for the season" verdict, judged against a clear-sky ceiling for your latitude and date. Panel-performance checks self-calibrate from your own history.

🗺️

Nearby, offline

Campsites, water, dump points, fuel and food from OpenStreetMap, cached locally for 7 days so it still works with no signal — arguably when you need it most.

🛰️

Daily SITREP

A Green / Amber / Red mission verdict derived from battery and weather, with plain-English recommendations: runtime left, heater-tonight, solar outlook.

📷

Camera & switches

Live USB-webcam view with snapshots saved on the Pi, and GPIO relay control wired in parallel with the van's manual switches.

🔼

Roof control

Press-and-hold buttons for the elevating roof, wired in parallel with the physical switch. A safety watchdog drops the relay the instant you let go — wifi or not.

🧭

Trips & Memories

Every journey leaves a breadcrumb trail on an offline map — distance, days on the road, and the start of a travel journal for the places you stop.

🌐

Remote & installable

Reach it anywhere over HTTPS via a Cloudflare Tunnel — no port forwarding, works behind CGNAT. Installable as a PWA on phone, tablet or dash.

Honest by design

VanOS never fabricates data. There's no battery percentage without a shunt fitted (it shows voltage instead), no gauges for sensors that aren't there. If a reading can't be measured, it isn't invented — the dashboard tells you what it actually knows, and what it doesn't.

Built on a plugin bus

Every data source — simulation today, a new sensor tomorrow — publishes onto one in-process telemetry bus in one schema. The UI subscribes once and never cares where a reading came from. Adding hardware means writing a small plugin, nothing else changes.
